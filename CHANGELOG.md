# Changelog

All notable changes to Window Light are recorded here.
The format follows Keep a Changelog, and versions follow Semantic Versioning.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Window Light opens with an empty wallpaper library that explains what a wallpaper is, in English or Polish, and quits when its window is closed
- Create a wallpaper from two to eight of your own images or videos: choose Create a Wallpaper… in the toolbar, press ⌘N, or drag the files into the window
- Add more images or videos to a wallpaper while it has empty times of day, or put one straight into an empty time of day
- JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF and raw photos are used exactly as they are, each with the time the camera recorded
- After every import a summary says how many pictures were added, which files were left out and why
- Window Light reads only the files you choose or drag in, and asks for no access to folders or your photo library
- A MOV or MP4 video gives one picture, taken from the middle of the video at full size, upright and with natural colours; the video itself is never copied or changed
- Stop a running import with Stop or the Escape key; pictures already added are kept
- Imported pictures go to the time of day they were taken; pictures without a capture time are spread evenly over the day
- An empty time of day shows the picture it borrows from another time of day, so the desktop always has something to show
- Move a picture to another time of day or swap two pictures, from the menu next to the picture
- Remove a picture from a wallpaper; the file it was imported from is never touched
- Place by Time Again in the toolbar arranges a wallpaper's pictures by the time they were taken once more, after asking
- Undo and redo every arranging step with Edit → Undo (⌘Z) and Redo (⇧⌘Z), for as long as the wallpaper stays open; a removed picture comes back with undo
